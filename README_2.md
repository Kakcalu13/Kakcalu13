## 🪶 Mission: *Mizo language technology*

Mizo is the language of my wife and our children — a Tibeto-Burman language of Northeast India. I'm still a learner. I can write Mizo, but **I cannot yet ask my computer to listen to it: there is no speech-to-text for Mizo.** No Siri, no Translate parity, almost no public datasets, almost no tooling.

So I am building it, one repository at a time. Many of these repos are even named in Mizo — small, deliberate acts of bringing the language onto GitHub:

| Repository | Mizo meaning | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [`tawng`](https://github.com/Kakcalu13) | *language / speak* | A mobile speak-and-listen app *(in development)* |
| [`Inkhelhna-ho-te`](https://github.com/Kakcalu13/Inkhelhna-ho-te) | *let's play* | A simple game, in Mizo, for the joy of it |
| [`duhthusam`](https://github.com/Kakcalu13) | *desire / will* | A C++ game engine built from first principles |
| [`mizo_ai_speech_to_text`](https://github.com/Kakcalu13/mizo_ai_speech_to_text) | — | A custom STT model for Mizo, trained on family voices |
| [`Mizo-godot-game`](https://github.com/Kakcalu13/Mizo-godot-game) | — | A Godot game teaching Mizo, English, and sign to my kids |

> **If you work on low-resource language NLP, speech models, or language preservation,** I would genuinely love to talk. The work is too important to do alone.
